Farever Gear Guide

Browse Farever gear drops and compare dungeon rewards with faction-shared sets using FareverMod.

Compare dungeon drops and shared gear

FareverMod’s Gear page brings dungeon gear into one view. Choose a faction and dungeon boss to see that boss’s drops alongside the faction’s shared set, making it easier to see where each piece comes from.

The companion also includes gear and enchant-planning tools for upgrades, infusions, elixirs, food and other build materials. Its gear information is drawn from Farever’s own game data.

FareverMod is a Windows companion app, not an online game service. Download it and explore the Gear page in the app.
